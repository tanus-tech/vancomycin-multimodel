Reference equations

Cockcroft-Gault (adults): CrCl = [(140 − Age) × Weight] / (72 × SCr) × 0.85 if female
IBWmale = 50 + 2.3 × (cm/2.54 − 60)  |  IBWfemale = 45.5 + 2.3 × (cm/2.54 − 60)
AdjBW = IBW + 0.4 × (ABW − IBW) — when ABW > 130% IBW
Adult standard: Goti et al. — TVCL 4.5 L/h, CrCl0.8 scaling
ARC: CrCl > 130 mL/min/1.73m² — Roberts et al. model applied
Pediatric (≥ 2 y): Abdulla et al. allometric model
Neonates (< 2 y / PMA given): Grimsley & Thomson / Allegaert model
